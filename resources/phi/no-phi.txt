The community garden has several raised beds filled with herbs, leafy vegetables, and bright seasonal flowers. A gravel path winds between the beds, and a wooden bench offers a quiet place to enjoy the shade. Rain barrels collect water from the shed roof so the plants can be watered during dry weather.

The gardening guide recommends loosening the soil before planting and spreading mulch to help retain moisture. Tools should be cleaned after use and stored in a dry place. At the end of each growing season, fallen leaves and plant trimmings can be added to the compost pile to enrich the soil for future planting.
